07 / THE PLAYBOOK

Good ideas.
Clear rules.

Make sure your team is ready before you arrive.

❖

Each team should consist of 2 members compulsory. A maximum of 5 teams from one institute can participate.

❖

For further clarifications, please contact Prof. Soumya Mirje – 7022894308.

❖

Each team should have at least 1 laptop compulsorily. The college will not provide a laptop or PC; participants are responsible for their laptops.

❖

There are 3 rounds in total, with eliminations after Round 2.

❖

Only registered teams are allowed to participate in the event.

❖

Participants must obey all event rules. A participating team may be disqualified for disobedience.

❖

A college ID card is mandatory for identification and verification.

❖

The last date to submit the registration form is 12th October before 12 PM.

❖

For elimination after Round 2, the combined scores from Round 1 and Round 2 will be considered. The Top 10 teams will proceed to the finals.

❖

For the General Championship (G.C.), the scores of all teams from a particular institute across Round 1, Round 2 and Round 3 will be added to determine the result.

❖

All rounds are surprise rounds related to “PROMPTPRENEUR.”

❖

The judge’s decision will be final and binding.

QUICK ANSWERS

FAQs

How many members can be in a team?＋

Each team must consist of exactly two members.

Can multiple teams participate from one institute?＋

Yes. A maximum of five teams may participate from one institute.

Do participants need to bring a laptop?＋

Yes. Every team must have at least one laptop. The college will not provide laptops or PCs.

How many rounds are there?＋

There are three surprise rounds related to PROMPTPRENEUR.

What happens after Round 2?＋

Teams are eliminated after Round 2. Combined scores from Rounds 1 and 2 determine the Top 10 teams advancing to Round 3.

What is required for identification?＋

A college ID card is mandatory.

When does registration close?＋

Registration closes on 12th October, before 12 PM.

Who should participants contact for clarification?＋

Contact event coordinator Prof. Soumya Mirje at 7022894308, or email klebbaank@gmail.com.
